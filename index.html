Privacy Policy

How Chain Reaction Max handles account details, game progress, age group, friends, multiplayer activity, advertising data and deletion requests.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Chain Reaction Max (Android package com.levilabs.chainreactionmax) is a board and strategy game published by Levi Labs (“Levi Labs”, “we”, “our” or “us”). This Privacy Policy describes how information is collected, used, shared, stored and deleted when you play the game, use its online features or contact us.

Some features, such as sign-in, purchases or online play, may be available only in certain versions or regions. Third-party services have their own privacy notices.

Privacy contact: levi000000007@gmail.com

2. Information we process

Age group

When the game first opens, it asks for a birth year. From it we work out an age group: under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over. We store the age group and the birth year you chose. We do not store your full date of birth. We use the age group to match players with the same age group in online games, to apply safe advertising settings, and to understand our audience in general terms. The age screen does not block any age group from playing.

Account and sign-in information

For cloud accounts signed in through Google or another enabled provider, we process the information needed for sign-in and account management. This may include your provider account identifier, Firebase user ID, display name, profile image, and email address where the provider shares it and you allow access. Sign-in providers and Firebase Authentication handle authentication; we never ask for your Google password inside the game.

Game profile and progress

We may store your player name, avatar, avatar frame, country or region setting, level, experience points (XP), game statistics, wins and games played, streaks, virtual coins and diamonds, rewards, owned cosmetics, entitlement status and related virtual-currency transaction records.

Multiplayer and social information

Online play may involve player IDs and names, friends and friend requests, room codes and invitations, matchmaking tickets, match participants, game moves and outcomes, preset emoji reactions, online or in-match status, and activity timestamps. Your public player name, avatar, level and status may be visible to other players as needed for online features. The game has no free-text chat. Players communicate only with a small set of preset emojis.

Guest and local information

Guest mode saves progress and settings on your device. This data can be lost if you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the game. Guest data is not sent to our cloud unless you sign in.

Advertising, device and diagnostic information

When ads are shown, Google AdMob and its partners may process IP address (which can indicate an approximate area), Android advertising ID or other device identifiers, ad views and clicks, device characteristics and diagnostic data, for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. These services collect data automatically through their SDKs. The game does not need your precise GPS location. We do not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics in the game at the time of this policy.

Purchases and payments

If you buy items, purchase and transaction status, purchased items and related identifiers are processed to deliver your purchase and prevent fraud. Payments are handled by Google Play. We do not receive your full payment card details.

Support requests

If you contact us, we process your message and contact details to answer you, investigate problems, or act on privacy requests. Please do not send passwords or payment card details.

3. How and why we use information

  • Sign you in and manage cloud accounts.
  • Save game profiles, progress, virtual items, rewards and purchases.
  • Connect players in online matches, friend rooms and invitations, and show online status and results.
  • Match players of the same age group and apply safer settings for younger players.
  • Show and measure ads, subject to your region’s rules and your choices.
  • Prevent impersonation, spam, abuse, cheating and fraud.
  • Find and fix technical problems and keep the game secure.
  • Answer questions, access, correction, objection and deletion requests, and follow the law.

Where a legal basis is required, it may be: providing the service you asked for, our legitimate interests in running and securing the game, legal obligations, or your consent (for example for personalised ads where the law requires consent). You can withdraw consent where the law gives you that right. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing.

4. Who receives information

We do not sell your game profile for money. Information may be processed or disclosed to these recipients, only as needed for the purposes above:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud: sign-in, cloud profiles, multiplayer records, database hosting and security.
  • Google AdMob and advertising partners: serving, measuring and securing ads. In some places advertising data sharing can count as a “share” or “sale” under privacy laws even when no money is paid to us.
  • Sign-in providers: Google, and others where enabled, under their own privacy policies.
  • Google Play: in-app purchases and entitlements.
  • Other players: your player name, avatar, level, status and match activity as needed for online play.
  • Authorities or professional advisers: when required by law or needed to protect rights and security.

More information: Firebase Privacy and Security, Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising technologies.

5. Advertising choices and consent

Ads may be personalised or non-personalised, depending on your location, device settings, age group, applicable law and your choices. Where consent is legally required, the game shows a consent message before ads are loaded, and you can change your choice later in Settings. For players in the under 13 and 13 to 17 age groups, we do not show personalised ads and we apply child-directed or under-age ad settings with a restricted ad content rating where available.

You can also manage your Android advertising ID in your device settings. Changing ad choices may not remove all ads.

6. How long we keep information

Cloud account information, profile data and game progress are kept while your account is active and as long as needed to run the game. Short-lived records such as matchmaking tickets, rooms and online status are kept only for as long as they are needed for play and are cleaned up regularly. Third-party SDKs may keep technical or advertising data under their own policies.

When you delete an account or make a valid deletion request, we delete or de-identify the personal data linked to that account as required by law. In a match with several players, a limited de-identified result may remain so other players’ records stay correct. Limited records may be kept where needed for legal, security, fraud-prevention, purchase-dispute, tax or accounting reasons. Provider backups and logs expire on their own schedules. Guest data stored only on your device can be removed by clearing the app’s storage or uninstalling the game.

7. Your controls: export and delete

  • Delete account: in the game open Settings → Delete Account. You may be asked to sign in again for security. Deleting your game account does not delete your separate Google account.
  • Export your data: in the game open Settings → Export My Data to save a copy of your profile information.
  • Without the app: follow the steps on our Account and Data Deletion page or email levi000000007@gmail.com. We may ask for reasonable proof that the account is yours.

For access, correction, objection or any other privacy request, use the same contact.

10. Children’s privacy

Chain Reaction Max is a simple board game that people of all ages, including children, may play. When the game first opens, it asks for a birth year to find the player’s age group (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over). We store only the age group and birth year, never the full date of birth, and we do not block any age group from playing.

For players in the under 13 and 13 to 17 groups: we do not show personalised ads, we apply child-directed and under-age ad settings with a restricted ad content rating where available, and players are matched in online games only with players of the same age group. Players choose a player name and avatar only; the game has no free-text chat (only a few preset emojis).

Parents or guardians who believe a child has given personal information without permission, or who want a child’s account and data deleted, can contact us at the privacy email or use the deletion page. We will review the request and delete the data as required by applicable law.
