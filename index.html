2. Information we process

Account and sign-in information

For cloud accounts signed in through Google, Facebook or another enabled sign-in provider, we process information necessary for sign-in and account management. This may include your provider account identifier, Firebase user ID, display name, profile image, and email address where the provider shares it and you authorize access. Sign-in providers and Firebase Authentication handle authentication information; we do not ask for your Google or Facebook password in our game.

Game profile and progress

We may store your selected player name, avatar, avatar frame, country or region setting if supplied, level, experience points (XP), game statistics, wins and losses, virtual coins and diamonds, rewards, cosmetics, achievements, entitlement status and relevant virtual-currency transaction records.

Multiplayer and social information

Online gameplay may involve player IDs and names, friends and friend requests, challenge invitations, room codes, match participants, matchmaking preferences, game moves and outcomes, online/offline or in-match presence, and activity timestamps. Your chosen public player name, avatar, level, and game status may be visible to other players as needed for multiplayer and social features. Game and match history may remain associated with other participants, subject to deletion and anonymization requests.

Guest and local information

Guest mode can save progress and preferences on your device (for example through local application storage). This information may be lost if you clear the app's data or uninstall it. If cloud sign-in is not used, local progress may not be synchronized to the cloud.

Advertising, device and diagnostic information

When ads are enabled, Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads and its advertising partners may process IP address (which can indicate approximate area), Android advertising or other device identifiers, app interactions, ad views and clicks, device characteristics, and app diagnostic and performance data for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. These services can collect data automatically through their software development kits. This does not mean the game needs precise GPS location.

Purchases and payments, if enabled

If purchases or ad-free entitlements are offered, purchase and transaction status, purchased items or entitlements and relevant identifiers may be processed to fulfil purchases and prevent fraud. Payment processing is handled by the relevant platform, such as Google Play; we do not need your complete payment-card details to provide game entitlements.

Support requests

If you contact us, we process your message, contact information and any information you choose to provide so we can answer, investigate issues, or act on privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, access tokens or payment-card details.

3. How and why we use information

  • Authenticate players and manage cloud accounts.
  • Store game profiles, progress, virtual items and purchases or rewards where enabled.
  • Connect players in online matches, friends rooms and challenges; display game presence and results.
  • Provide requested social features and prevent impersonation, spam, abuse, account misuse and fraud.
  • Show and measure advertisements when enabled, subject to applicable privacy choices.
  • Diagnose technical problems, maintain security and improve game reliability.
  • Respond to questions, access, correction, objection and deletion requests, and comply with applicable laws.

For people in regions where a legal basis is required, the relevant bases may include providing the service requested by you, legitimate interests in securing and operating the game where permitted, compliance with legal obligations, and consent for particular uses such as personalised advertising when required by law. You can withdraw consent where the law provides that right; withdrawal does not invalidate previous lawful processing.

4. Who receives information

We do not sell your game account profile for money. Information may be processed or disclosed to the following categories of recipients, only as needed for the purposes described:

  • Google Firebase and Google Cloud — sign-in, cloud profiles, multiplayer records, database hosting, security and related infrastructure.
  • Google AdMob and advertising technology providers — serving, measuring and securing advertisements, including through advertising identifiers and technical data. Advertising data sharing may be considered a "share" or "sale" under some privacy laws even when no money is paid to us for user profiles.
  • Sign-in platforms — Google, Facebook/Meta and Google Play Games where the corresponding login method is enabled, under their respective privacy policies.
  • Google Play and payment providers — processing in-app purchases or entitlements where enabled.
  • Other players — your display name, avatar, status, room/match participation and relevant in-game activity when necessary for online play.
  • Authorities or professional advisers — when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights and security, subject to applicable law.

Relevant third-party information is available at Firebase Privacy & Security, Google Privacy Policy, Google advertising technologies and Meta Privacy Policy.

5. Advertising choices and consent

Advertisements may be personalised or non-personalised, depending on your location, device settings, applicable law, the advertising service and any consent choices presented to you. In regions where consent is legally required, we must obtain and respect appropriate consent before the relevant advertising processing takes place.

You can manage your Android advertising identifier and available privacy preferences in your device settings. You may also contact Levi Labs to exercise any applicable rights concerning targeted advertising, sale or sharing of personal data. Changing advertising preferences may not remove all advertisements.

6. How long we keep information

Cloud account information, profile data and game progress are generally held while an account remains active and for as long as reasonably needed to provide the game or meet disclosed purposes. Short-lived match, room, presence and diagnostic information may have different operational retention periods. Third-party SDKs may retain technical or advertising data according to their policies.

When you delete an account or make a valid deletion request, we will delete or de-identify eligible personal data associated with that account as required by law, including cloud identity and profile records. Where a match involves several players, we may retain a limited de-identified result or other information necessary for other users' records. Limited records may be kept when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, purchase-dispute, tax or accounting reasons. Provider backups and logs may expire on their own schedules.

Guest-mode data stored only on your device can normally be removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.
