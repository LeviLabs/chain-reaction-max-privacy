Account & Data Deletion

How to delete your Chain Reaction Max game account or request removal of associated personal information, even without the app installed.

Account deletion options

You may request deletion of your Chain Reaction Max account and associated personal game data whether or not you currently have the app installed. Your separate Google or Facebook account will not be deleted by this request.

Option 1: Request deletion inside the game

  1. Open Chain Reaction Max and sign in to the account you want deleted.
  2. Open Settings and select Delete Account.
  3. Review the warning and confirm your choice.
  4. If instructed, sign out and sign in again to verify your account, then retry deletion.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

  1. Email the privacy contact below from the address linked to your account, if available.
  2. Use the subject Chain Reaction Max — Delete My Account.
  3. Include your in-game player name and the login method you used (Google, Facebook, or another supported option). Do not include your password.
  4. We may verify account ownership before processing the request. We will respond and process verified requests within applicable legal deadlines.

Email an account deletion request

If the email button does not work, send your request manually to REPLACE_WITH_YOUR_EMAIL@example.com.

What deletion covers

Deletion of an authenticated game account is intended to remove or de-identify personal data controlled by Levi Labs that is associated with that account, as required by applicable law. This includes:

  • Game account and associated Firebase sign-in identity.
  • Online profile name, selected avatar and avatar frame, game levels, XP, virtual coins, diamonds, rewards and statistics.
  • Friend connections, requests and invitations.
  • Presence, room membership and personal game records associated with the account, subject to shared match and legal exceptions.
  • Eligible transaction and entitlement records, subject to any legally required financial, fraud-prevention or dispute records.

What may be retained

We may retain only information that applicable law permits or requires, such as transaction or security records, data needed for active disputes, or minimal de-identified match results that are necessary for other players' records. Service-provider backups and system logs may be removed on their standard schedules. We do not keep active personal profiles simply to avoid completing deletion.

For the treatment of guest-mode data, see the Privacy Policy; guest data stored locally can generally be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.
