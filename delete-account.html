Account & Data Deletion

How to delete your Chain Reaction Max account or ask us to remove your personal information, even without the app installed.

Android gameApp ID: com.levilabs.chainreactionmaxLast updated: November 17, 2026

Option 1: Delete inside the game

  1. Open Chain Reaction Max and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. On the Home screen tap the Settings (gear) button.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the warning and confirm.
  5. If the game asks you to sign in again, do it and tap delete again. This keeps your account safe.

Your separate Google account is not deleted. Purchase history kept by Google Play stays with Google.

Option 2: Ask without the app

  1. Send an email to levi000000007@gmail.com, if possible from the email linked to your account.
  2. Use the subject Chain Reaction Max - Delete My Account.
  3. Include your in-game player name and the sign-in method you used (for example Google). Never send your password.
  4. We may ask for reasonable proof that the account is yours. Then we process the request within the time the law allows.
Email a deletion request

For parents and guardians

If your child plays Chain Reaction Max and you want their account and data deleted, email us at the address above. Write “Parent or guardian request” in the subject, and include the child’s player name. We will confirm and delete the account data as required by applicable law.

What gets deleted

  • Your game account and Firebase sign-in identity.
  • Your profile: player name, avatar, frame, country setting, age group, level, XP, coins, diamonds, rewards, statistics and owned items.
  • Your currency transaction history, daily reward progress, ad-free reward state and purchase records kept by the game.
  • Your player-name reservation, online status, current room, matchmaking ticket and rooms you host.
  • Your friend connections, friend requests and room invitations.
  • Game data saved on your device.

What may be kept

We keep only what the law allows or requires: for example transaction or security records, data needed for an open dispute, and minimal de-identified match results that other players’ records need. Backups and system logs are removed on their normal schedules. We do not keep an active profile just to avoid completing a deletion.

Guest data

Guest progress is stored only on your device. Clear the app’s storage or uninstall the game to remove it.
